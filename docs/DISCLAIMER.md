# Disclaimer & Compliance Stance

**Project Atlas AI is an investment _research_ tool. It is not investment advice.**

- Nothing produced by this platform is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any
  security, nor a solicitation or offer to do so.
- The platform makes **no guarantee of returns** and issues **no deterministic
  predictions**. Every output is probabilistic and is accompanied by an explicit
  confidence level, stated assumptions, known risks, and supporting evidence.
- Markets are uncertain. Past performance and historical analogues do not
  guarantee future outcomes.
- Users are solely responsible for their own investment decisions and should
  consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting.
- Automated order execution, where offered in future phases, is performed **only
  with explicit, per-action user authorization** and under configurable risk
  controls. The platform respects all applicable regulations and the terms of any
  broker API it integrates with.

Every recommendation object generated by the platform embeds this disclaimer in
its payload (`recommendation.disclaimer`) and is written to an immutable audit
trail together with the model version, prompt version, timestamp, and a summary
of the reasoning that produced it.
